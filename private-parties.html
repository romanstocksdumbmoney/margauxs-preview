The Bordeaux Room

Accommodate up to 46 people for a sit-down dinner. Completely self-contained private dining room with its own kitchen, lavatory, dining room & bar. A 25-person minimum & one check is required. A $500 non-refundable deposit secures the room ($1,000 in December), applied to your final bill.

For groups of 25 or more, a 3-Course Prix-Fixe Menu is designed for you. Prices begin at $89.95 per person.

Up to 46 · from $89.95 pp

The Rotisserie Room

Accommodate 12 to 18 people for a semi-private dining experience. A $250 non-refundable deposit is required ($500 in December), applied to your final bill.

Chef Pettifer and his team execute a 3-Course Prix-Fixe package starting at $89.95 pp.

12–18 guests · from $89.95 pp

The Entire Restaurant

Since Margaux's is closed to the public on Sundays, we make our entire restaurant available for large events (150–275 people) with full-service catering. Includes Rotisserie and Bordeaux rooms, spacious bar area, and main dining room with space for a band and dance floor.

Sundays · 150–275 guests
